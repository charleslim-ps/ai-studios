# Research Lab Benchmark

The Partnership Benchmark quiz on Research Lab: the visitor answers 5 questions, then gets a benchmark
card (score ring + 4 tiles) and an opening chat message from Dr. Stack, a devs.ai agent.

There are no live data calls. Every number is a quarterly Looker snapshot baked into the agent
instructions, and the app sends only the quiz answers.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `agent-instructions.md` | Part 1: the static devs.ai prompt with every lookup table. Part 2: the per-session input. Notes for the team. |
| `scripts/build_tables.py` | Regenerates the lookup tables (2a, 2b, 2d) from three Looker pulls. |
| `data/` | The raw Looker pulls. **Gitignored**, because they hold per-vendor figures. |

Design source: Figma *Research Lab 2026 Q4 (FR-FR)*: question tree `51:9698`, prompt `144:2`,
results console `180:534`.

## Quarterly refresh (Mar / Jun / Sep / Dec 20)

1. Run the three Looker queries listed at the top of `scripts/build_tables.py` and save each result
   to `data/` under the file names shown there.
2. `python3 scripts/build_tables.py > tables.md`
3. Paste each section over the matching section of `agent-instructions.md` (2a, 2b, 2d), and update
   the "Data as of" line.
4. Re-check the figures in the glossary below that aren't generated by the script.

## Glossary: the network figures

Generated by `build_tables.py`:

- **Partner count percentile.** Where the visitor's partner count sits among vendors in their category,
  counting distinct partnerships per vendor. 1,989 vendors, median 175 network-wide.
- **Partner revenue percentile.** Where their 2025 partner-driven revenue sits among 811 vendors.
  Vendors that pay commissions but don't report revenue to PartnerStack are left out, because their
  revenue is unknown, not zero.
- **Benchmark score.** Composite rank: each of 772 vendors is scored 50% partner count percentile +
  50% revenue percentile, and the visitor's score is where they'd rank.
- **Revenue per partner.** Partner revenue ÷ partner count, ranked against the 493 vendors with revenue.
- **Category momentum.** Growth in 2025 partner commissions vs 2024, by quiz category.

Pulled by hand from Looker on 2026-09-23 (not in the script yet):

- **81,564 marketplace joins.** Partners who joined 584 vendors through the PartnerStack marketplace
  in 2025 (`partnership.is_marketplace`). Counter-stat for the "No bandwidth" blocker.
- **$7.46 per $1 of commission.** 2025 partner-driven revenue ÷ commissions paid, for vendor-months
  with reported revenue ($881.6M ÷ $118.2M). Counter-stat for "Cannot prove ROI".

Carried over from earlier data-team work (not re-pulled; source per the Figma data map):

- **39.6% vs 3.8%.** Earning rate: the share of partners who earn at least one commission. 39.6% for
  vetted partners who came through the PartnerStack network, 3.8% for partners from outside it
  (10.4x). Data Request slide 31. Correlation only: vetted partners earn more; the network doesn't
  cause it.
- **23.2%.** Average commission rate offered by the top 25 vendors. RL-37.
- **40.4%.** Share of all network commissions paid on offers with a 20%+ commission rate. RL-37.
- **+62.8%.** Vendors running 2+ partner programs (e.g. affiliate + referral) have 62.8% more partner
  revenue than single-program vendors. Data Request slide 24. An association, not a promise.
- **0 days / 74.5%.** Median time from a vendor's launch to its first partnership is 0 days, and 74.5%
  land one within 10 days. Data Request slide 28.
